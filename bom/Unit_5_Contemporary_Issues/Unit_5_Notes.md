## 1. Business Process Reengineering (BPR)

### Concept & Definition (Michael Hammer & James Champy)
*"The fundamental rethinking and radical redesign of business processes to achieve dramatic improvements in critical, contemporary measures of performance, such as cost, quality, service, and speed."*

### The 4 Keywords of BPR
- **Fundamental:** Asking why we do what we do.
- **Radical:** Rebuilding processes from their root rather than making superficial modifications.
- **Dramatic:** Targeting 70–90% performance leaps instead of incremental 5–10% gains.
- **Processes:** Organizing around complete customer journeys rather than functional silos.

### Success Factors vs. Failure Reasons
- **Success Factors:** Top executive sponsorship, clear process mapping, leveraging IT as an enabler, empathetic change management.
- **Failure Reasons:** Using BPR as a mask for headcount downsizing, employee resistance to cultural change, retreating into incrementalism.

## 2. Learning Organisation (Peter Senge)

### Meaning
An organisation that continuously expands its capacity to create its future, facilitating the learning of all its members and continuously transforming itself.

### The Five Disciplines (Peter Senge’s *The Fifth Discipline*)
1. **Systems Thinking (The Cornerstone):** Viewing the enterprise as an integrated web of interrelationships.
2. **Personal Mastery:** A personal commitment to continuous personal growth and skill refinement.
3. **Mental Models:** Surfacing, testing, and dismantling deeply ingrained internal assumptions.
4. **Building Shared Vision:** Fostering a genuine, collective commitment to a shared future.
5. **Team Learning:** Dialogic collaboration and thinking together, transcending individual egos.

### Barriers & Problems in Implementation
- Deep-seated bureaucratic habits and fear of change.
- Punitive corporate cultures that penalize honest experiment failures.
- Lack of dedicated time and resources for reflective learning.

## 3. Six Sigma Quality Management

### Concept & Origin
- Pioneered by **Bill Smith** at Motorola in 1986; scaled globally by **Jack Welch** at General Electric.
- **Statistical Benchmark:** No more than **3.4 defects per million opportunities (DPMO)** ($99.99966\%$ accuracy).

### The DMAIC Methodology
```
[ DEFINE ] ──► [ MEASURE ] ──► [ ANALYZE ] ──► [ IMPROVE ] ──► [ CONTROL ]
```
- **Define:** Identify the problem and customer quality requirements (Critical-to-Quality).
- **Measure:** Map current process performance and gather defect metrics.
- **Analyze:** Pinpoint the root cause of defects (using Pareto charts, Ishikawa fishbone diagrams).
- **Improve:** Formulate, test, and implement targeted process solutions.
- **Control:** Maintain the gains using statistical process control (SPC) charts and updated SOPs.

## 4. Supply Chain Management (SCM)

### Concept
The systematic coordination of procurement, production, inventory management, warehousing, and transportation from raw material origin to the end customer.

### The Five Basic Stages of SCM (SCOR Model)
```
 [ PLAN ] ──► [ SOURCE ] ──► [ MAKE ] ──► [ DELIVER ] ──► [ RETURN ]
```
- **Plan:** Forecasting demand and optimizing resource requirements.
- **Source:** Selecting vendors, procuring raw materials, and managing contracts.
- **Make:** Manufacturing, testing, packaging, and assembling products.
- **Deliver:** Logistics, inventory routing, order fulfillment, and distribution.
- **Return:** Handling defective products, warranty services, and reverse recycling.

## 5. Modern Workplace Trends

### A. Work-Life Balance
- Achieving an equitable equilibrium between career ambitions and personal life (health, family, leisure).
- **Corporate Value:** Reduces chronic employee burnout, lowers absenteeism, and retains high-performing talent.

### B. Freelancing & The Gig Economy
- Self-employed specialists delivering project-based services to multiple clients.
- **Types:** Independent Contractors, Moonlighters, Diversified Workers, Gig Taskers, Solopreneurs.

### C. Flexi-Time & Work From Home (WFH)
```
┌────────────────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────────┐
│               BENEFITS                 │              CHALLENGES                │
├────────────────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ - Commute time eliminated              │ - Blurred boundaries & digital burnout │
│ - Personalized work-hour schedule      │ - Social isolation & loneliness        │
│ - Access to global talent pools        │ - Team coordination across time zones  │
│ - Significant office rent savings      │ - Data security & cybersecurity risks  │
└────────────────────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### D. Co-Sharing / Co-Working Spaces (e.g., WeWork, Awfis)
- Shared professional workspace where freelancers, remote employees, and startups rent desks and meeting rooms with shared amenities.
- **Advantages:** Low capital commitment, flexible leases, enterprise-grade internet and facilities, vibrant cross-industry networking.
