## 2. Decision-Making: Meaning, Process, & Techniques

### Herbert Simon’s Bounded Rationality
- Classical economic theory assumed "perfect rationality" (homo economicus).
- **Herbert Simon** demonstrated that human decision-makers operate under **Bounded Rationality**:
  1. Imperfect and incomplete information.
  2. Cognitive and computational human limitations.
  3. Time and financial constraints.
- **Outcome:** Managers do not achieve optimal solutions; they engage in **"Satisficing"** (choosing an alternative that meets the threshold of adequacy).

## 4. Centralisation vs. Decentralisation & Delegation

### The Triad of Delegation
```
          ┌──────────────────────────────────────────┐
          │             1. AUTHORITY                 │
          │     (Right to command; Flows DOWN)       │
          └─────────────────────┬────────────────────┘
                                │
          ┌─────────────────────▼────────────────────┐
          │            2. RESPONSIBILITY             │
          │    (Obligation to perform; Flows UP)     │
          └─────────────────────┬────────────────────┘
                                │
          ┌─────────────────────▼────────────────────┐
          │            3. ACCOUNTABILITY             │
          │   (Answerability; CANNOT be delegated)   │
          └──────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
- **Principle of Absoluteness of Accountability:** A manager can delegate authority and responsibility to a subordinate, but remains 100% accountable to higher management for the final outcome.

## 5. Organisational Structures & Organograms

### A. Line & Staff Structure
- Line managers hold direct operational authority along the scalar chain.
- Staff specialists provide expert advice, legal counsel, and technical research without direct operational command authority.

### B. Divisional / Product Structure
- The organization is divided into autonomous semi-independent divisions based on product lines (e.g., Tata Motors, Tata Steel, Tata Consumer).
- Each division contains its own functional units (R&D, Sales, Finance).

### C. Matrix Organisation Structure
- Combines functional departments with horizontal project teams.
- **Dual Command:** Every specialist reports to a Functional Head and a Project Head.
- **Challenge:** Violates Unity of Command; causes dual accountability conflicts.

### D. Virtual Organisation
- A boundaryless network of independent companies, contractors, and specialists linked electronically via information technology.
- Characterized by minimal fixed physical offices, low overhead, and high agile collaboration.
