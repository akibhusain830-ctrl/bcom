# Business Organisation and Management (BOM)
**B.Com. Semester 1 | UGCBCS | 4 Credits**

---

## Course Objectives & Learning Outcomes
- **Objective:** Provide foundational knowledge regarding the organisation and management of a business enterprise.
- **Outcome:** Understand organizational structures and processes; develop knowledge and skills in management principles and operational functions.

---

## Detailed Syllabus Overview

### [Unit 1: Introduction](./Unit_1_Introduction/) (16 Classes)
- **Nature and Purpose of Business:** Meaning, features, objectives.
- **Starting a Business:** Key factors to consider.
- **Forms of Business Organisation:** Sole proprietorship, Partnership, LLC/LLP, Joint Stock Company, Cooperative societies.
- **Modern Business Formats:**
  - Brick & Mortar
  - Brick & Click
  - E-commerce
  - Franchising
  - Outsourcing (BPO/KPO)
- **Management Foundations:**
  - Nature and Functions of Management (POSDCORB overview)
  - Managerial Competencies & Roles

---

### [Unit 2: Business Environment](./Unit_2_Business_Environment/) (8 Classes)
- **Meaning & Significance:** Concept and impact of business environment.
- **Layers of Business Environment:**
  - Micro / Immediate (Customers, competitors, suppliers, intermediaries)
  - Meso / Intermediate (Industry and sector-specific environment)
  - Macro (PESTLE - Political, Economic, Socio-cultural, Technological, Legal, Environmental)
  - International / Global environment
- **Ethics & Governance:** Business ethics and Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR).

---

### [Unit 3: Planning and Organizing](./Unit_3_Planning_and_Organizing/) (12 Classes)
- **Planning:**
  - Strategic Planning (Vision, Mission, Objectives, SWOT)
  - Decision-Making: Steps in the decision-making process, quantitative and qualitative techniques.
- **Organizing:**
  - Formal vs. Informal Organisations
  - Authority & Power: Centralisation vs. Decentralisation, Delegation of authority (Principles & barriers).
  - Factors affecting organizational design.
  - **Organisational Structures & Organograms:**
    - Divisional structure
    - Product structure
    - Matrix structure
    - Project structure
    - Virtual organisation

---

### [Unit 4: Directing and Controlling](./Unit_4_Directing_and_Controlling/) (12 Classes)
- **Motivation:** Meaning, importance, and factors affecting motivation (Maslow, Herzberg, McGregor basics).
- **Leadership:** Concept, importance, trait theories, and leadership styles (Autocratic, Democratic, Laissez-faire, Transformational).
- **Communication:** Communication process, barriers, new trends and directions (Role of IT, enterprise communication tools, social media).
- **Controlling:**
  - Principles and process of controlling.
  - Techniques and measures of controlling.
  - Relationship and feedback loop between planning and controlling.

---

### [Unit 5: Contemporary Issues in Management](./Unit_5_Contemporary_Issues/) (12 Classes)
- **Modern Management Paradigms:**
  - Business Process Reengineering (BPR)
  - Learning Organisation (Peter Senge's concept)
  - Six Sigma (DMAIC methodology)
  - Supply Chain Management (SCM)
- **Future of Work & Emerging Workplace Trends:**
  - Work-Life Balance
  - Freelancing & Gig Economy
  - Flexi-time and Work From Home (WFH) / Remote work
  - Co-sharing and Co-working spaces

---
