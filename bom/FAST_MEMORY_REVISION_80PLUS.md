## 💡 How University Examiners Award Marks (The Secret)
Examiners do **NOT** read long paragraphs. They check answers in **30 to 45 seconds** using this mental checklist:
1. Did the student write the **correct bold heading / keyword**?
2. Is the explanation **clean and easy to understand** (2 simple sentences)?
3. Is there a **neat box, arrow, or diagram**?
4. Is there a **simple real-life example**?

If yes $\rightarrow$ **Full marks are awarded!** You do not need complicated words. Simple English with the right keywords gets higher marks than confusing English.

## 🎯 UNIT 1: Introduction & Business Formats

### 1. Factors to Consider When Starting a Business (10 Marks / 5 Marks)
* **Memory Trick (Mnemonic):** **"L-S-F-L-C-P-W"** $\rightarrow$ *(**L**earn **S**ome **F**acts, **L**et **C**ash **P**ay **W**orkers)*
1. **Line of Business (What to sell):** Choose the product or service based on customer demand and market research.
2. **Size of Firm (How big):** Decide whether to start small or large, depending on money available and market size.
3. **Form of Ownership (Legal type):** Choose Sole Proprietorship (one owner), Partnership, or Company (private/public).
4. **Location (Where to open):** Pick a place close to raw materials, transport, labor, and customers.
5. **Capital Requirement (Money needed):** Arrange **Fixed Capital** (machinery, building) and **Working Capital** (daily cash, salaries).
6. **Physical Facilities (Equipment):** Buy machines, computers, and design a smooth factory/shop layout.
7. **Workforce (Employees):** Hire skilled managers and workers to do the daily work.

---

### 2. Modern Business Formats (Comparison Made Super Easy)
* **Brick & Mortar:** Only a physical shop. You go inside, touch the product, and buy it immediately. *(Example: Local Kirana store / Bata outlet)*
* **Brick & Click:** Physical shop + Online app/website. You can buy online and pick up in store, or buy in store and get home delivery. *(Example: Reliance Digital, Croma, Walmart)*
* **Pure E-Commerce:** 100% online on website/app. No customer visit physical stores. *(Example: Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho)*
* **Franchising:** The owner (**Franchisor**) sells the right to use their brand name, recipe, and system to an entrepreneur (**Franchisee**) for an upfront fee and a percentage of monthly sales (royalty). *(Example: McDonald’s, Domino’s, Subway)*
  - *Advantage for Franchisor:* Expands quickly across cities without spending own money on building stores.
  - *Advantage for Franchisee:* Easy to make money from Day 1 because the brand is already famous.

---

### 3. Functions of Management (5 Marks)
* **Memory Trick:** **P - O - S - D - C** *(Please Open School During Corona)*
```
 [ Planning ] ──► [ Organizing ] ──► [ Staffing ] ──► [ Directing ] ──► [ Controlling ]
```
1. **Planning:** Thinking before doing. Deciding what to do, how to do it, and when to do it.
2. **Organizing:** Dividing work into departments and assigning duties to people.
3. **Staffing:** Finding the right person for the right job (recruiting, hiring, training).
4. **Directing:** Guiding, motivating, leading, and instructing employees to work well.
5. **Controlling:** Checking if actual work matches the planned goals, and fixing any mistakes.

---

### 4. Managerial Competencies / Skills (5 Marks)
* **Concept by Robert Katz:**
1. **Technical Skills:** Knowing *how* to do specific jobs (coding, accounting, machine repair). Most needed by **Lower-level supervisors**.
2. **Human / Interpersonal Skills:** Knowing *how to deal with people* (good communication, teamwork, motivation). Needed **Equally at all levels**.
3. **Conceptual Skills:** The ability to see the *big picture* and plan for future years. Most needed by **Top-level executives (CEOs)**.

## 🎯 UNIT 2: Business Environment & CSR

### 1. Layers of Business Environment (10 Marks / 5 Marks)
* **Memory Trick:** **Micro** = Close | **Meso** = Industry | **Macro** = Whole Country | **International** = World
```
   ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
   │ 4. INTERNATIONAL : World trade, WTO, foreign currency       │
   │  ┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐  │
   │  │ 3. MACRO (PESTLE) : Politics, Economy, Society, Tech  │  │
   │  │  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────┐  │  │
   │  │  │ 2. MESO : Industry groups (CII, FICCI, NASSCOM) │  │  │
   │  │  │  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────┐  │  │  │
   │  │  │  │ 1. MICRO : Customers, Suppliers, Rivals  │  │  │  │
   │  │  │  │              [ THE BUSINESS ]            │  │  │  │
   │  │  │  └──────────────────────────────────────────┘  │  │  │
   │  │  └─────────────────────────────────────────────────┘  │  │
   │  └───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘  │
   └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
1. **Micro Environment (Immediate):** People and groups who deal with the company directly every single day.
   - *Key players:* Customers, Suppliers, Competitors, Distributors.
2. **Meso Environment (Intermediate):** The industry level.
   - *Key players:* Industry associations (like NASSCOM for software, CII) and sector supply chains.
3. **Macro Environment (General / PESTLE):** Big outside forces that affect all companies in the country:
   - **P - Political:** Government policy, political stability.
   - **E - Economic:** Inflation, bank interest rates, income levels.
   - **S - Social:** Culture, lifestyle trends, population changes.
   - **T - Technological:** New software, smartphones, automation.
   - **L - Legal:** Laws and government regulations.
   - **E - Environmental:** Pollution rules, green energy.
4. **International Environment:** Global forces outside India (global trade treaties, dollar exchange rate, wars).

### 2. Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR): Archie Carroll's 4 Pillars (10 Marks / 5 Marks)
* **Memory Trick:** **E - L - E - P** *(**E**very **L**eader **E**arns **P**rofit)*
```
        ▲
       / \       4. PHILANTHROPIC : Charity, building schools, helping community
      /───\      3. ETHICAL       : Do what is fair and right (even if no law forces you)
     /─────\     2. LEGAL         : Follow the laws and pay all taxes on time
    /───────\    1. ECONOMIC      : Make profit (Foundational base for survival)
   /─────────\
```
1. **Economic Duty (The Base):** Earn profit by producing useful goods so the business can survive and create jobs.
2. **Legal Duty:** Obey all government rules, labor laws, and pay fair taxes.
3. **Ethical Duty:** Do what is morally right, treat workers fairly, and do not mislead customers with false ads.
4. **Philanthropic Duty (The Top):** Donate money for education, hospitals, sports, and disaster relief (e.g., Tata building cancer hospitals).

---

### 3. Business Responsibilities to Different Groups (5 Marks / 10 Marks)
1. **To Shareholders / Owners:** Protect their invested money and give good profit dividends.
2. **To Employees / Workers:** Pay fair wages, provide safe working conditions, and respect them.
3. **To Customers:** Give good quality products at honest prices, and listen to complaints.
4. **To Government:** Pay taxes honestly and never do corruption or bribery.
5. **To Society / Public:** Do not pollute water and air; help improve local villages and towns.

## 🎯 UNIT 3: Planning & Organizing

### 1. Steps in Decision-Making (5 Marks — Repeated 2023, 2024, 2025!)
* **Memory Trick:** **"I - D - A - E - S - I - F"** $\rightarrow$ *(**I** **D**ecide **A**nd **E**very **S**tep **I**s **F**ine)*
1. **Identify the Problem:** Notice what is going wrong (e.g., sales are dropping).
2. **Diagnose Root Causes:** Find out *why* it is happening (e.g., product quality or bad marketing?).
3. **Develop Alternatives:** Brainstorm 3 or 4 different ways to solve it.
4. **Evaluate Alternatives:** Compare each choice by cost, risk, and time needed.
5. **Select the Best Option:** Pick the solution that gives maximum result with minimum risk.
6. **Implement the Choice:** Put the decision into action with real workers and money.
7. **Follow-up & Feedback:** Check if the decision worked, and make corrections if needed.

---

### 2. What is 'Bounded Rationality'? (2 Marks / 5 Marks)
- **Thinker:** **Herbert Simon**.
- **Simple Meaning:** Human beings can never make 100% perfect decisions because:
  1. We do not have all information.
  2. Our human brain has limited calculating power.
  3. We have limited time.
- **Result:** Managers do not search for the "perfect" solution; they pick a **"Satisficing"** (good enough) solution.

---

### 3. The 3 Elements of Delegation (5 Marks)
* **Memory Trick:** **A - R - A** *(Authority, Responsibility, Accountability)*
```
 1. AUTHORITY      ──► Right to command and make decisions (Flows DOWN)
 2. RESPONSIBILITY ──► Duty to do the assigned work (Flows UP)
 3. ACCOUNTABILITY ──► Final answerability for the result (CANNOT be passed to anyone!)
```
- **Golden Rule to Write:** You can delegate authority and work, but **Accountability can NEVER be delegated**. If your subordinate fails, YOU are still answerable to your boss.

---

### 4. Matrix Organisation Structure (5 Marks)
- **Simple Meaning:** A hybrid structure where an employee has **TWO BOSSES** at the same time:
  1. **Functional Manager** (head of department, e.g., Head of Engineering).
  2. **Project Manager** (head of specific project, e.g., Project Alpha).
- **Advantage:** Fast work and sharing of experts between projects.
- **Problem (Dual Accountability):** Confusing for employees because both bosses give different orders at the same time!

---

### 5. Virtual Organisation (2 Marks — Repeated 2023, 2024, 2025!)
- **Definition:** A company that exists mostly on the internet and computers without big physical buildings.
- **How it works:** It connects independent suppliers, designers, and freelancers through software and cloud networks.
- **Key Advantage:** Very low rent/office costs and high flexibility.

## 🎯 UNIT 4: Directing & Controlling

### 1. Maslow's Hierarchy of Human Needs (5 Marks / 10 Marks)
* **Memory Trick:** **"P - S - S - E - S"** *(**P**lease **S**ave **S**ome **E**xtra **S**nacks)*
```
        ▲
       / \       5. SELF-ACTUALIZATION : Becoming the best you can be (Personal dream)
      /───\      4. ESTEEM NEEDS       : Respect, title, praise, status
     /─────\     3. SOCIAL / BELONGING : Friends, love, team acceptance
    /───────\    2. SAFETY & SECURITY  : Job security, health insurance, savings
   /─────────\   1. PHYSIOLOGICAL      : Food, water, shelter, basic salary
```
- **Core Rule:** Once a lower need is satisfied, it stops motivating. The person only works harder for the next higher need.

---

### 2. Douglas McGregor’s Theory X and Theory Y (5 Marks / 10 Marks)
* **Theory X (Negative view of workers):**
  - Assumes workers are lazy, dislike work, and want to avoid responsibility.
  - Managers must use strict rules, warnings, and constant punishment.
* **Theory Y (Positive view of workers):**
  - Assumes work is as natural as play or rest.
  - People like taking responsibility and are creative when respected.
  - Managers act like coaches and give freedom and encouragement.

---

### 3. Leadership Styles (5 Marks / 10 Marks)
1. **Autocratic (Boss-Centred):**
   - The leader makes all decisions alone and gives orders. Subordinates have no say.
   - *Good in:* Military, emergencies, or with uneducated/careless labor.
2. **Democratic (Participative):**
   - The leader invites team members to discuss, vote, and give ideas before deciding.
   - *Good in:* Offices, tech firms, creative teams. Increases team happiness.
3. **Laissez-Faire (Free-Rein / Hands-off):**
   - The leader gives complete freedom to the workers. Workers make their own goals.
   - *Good in:* Teams of high-level experts, research scientists, software engineers.

---

### 4. Relationship Between Planning and Controlling (2 Marks / 5 Marks — 100% Repeated!)
* **Write this golden quote:**  
  *"Planning without controlling is meaningless; controlling without planning is blind."*
* **3 Simple Points:**
  1. Planning sets the **target** (e.g., produce 1,000 units).
  2. Controlling **measures** actual output (e.g., we produced 900 units) and finds the gap.
  3. Planning is **looking ahead** (future); controlling is **looking back** (past performance) to fix mistakes.

## 🎯 UNIT 5: Contemporary Issues

### 1. Business Process Reengineering (BPR) (5 Marks / 10 Marks)
* **Simple Definition:** Throwing away old, slow ways of working and completely redesigning work processes from scratch to get massive jumps in speed, cost, and quality.
* **4 Core Words:**
  1. **Fundamental:** Asking *"Why are we doing this?"*
  2. **Radical:** Starting from zero, not making small adjustments.
  3. **Dramatic:** Aiming for huge results (80% faster), not small 5% changes.
  4. **Processes:** Focusing on the customer's journey from order to delivery.

---

### 2. Six Sigma Quality (1 Mark / 2 Marks / 5 Marks)
- **Inventor:** **Bill Smith** at Motorola in 1986 (made famous by Jack Welch at GE).
- **Standard:** Near-zero defects $\rightarrow$ Maximum **3.4 defects per million** opportunities ($99.99966\%$ accuracy).
- **Method (DMAIC):**
  - **D** - Define the problem.
  - **M** - Measure the current defects.
  - **A** - Analyze why defects happen.
  - **I** - Improve by fixing the root cause.
  - **C** - Control and maintain the high quality.

---

### 3. Flexi-Time & Work From Home (WFH) (5 Marks / 10 Marks)
* **Benefits:**
  1. Saves 2–3 hours of traffic and travel daily.
  2. Flexibility to balance family and personal life.
  3. Companies save huge money on office rent and electricity.
* **Problems:**
  1. Can feel lonely and isolated from teammates.
  2. Hard to stop working, causing mental burnout.
  3. Internet and computer security problems.

---

### 4. Co-Working Spaces (2 Marks)
- **Simple Meaning:** An office where workers from different companies, startups, and freelancers share desks, Wi-Fi, meeting rooms, and coffee machines.
- **Why popular:** Cheap monthly rent, no long leases, and great chance to make business contacts. *(Examples: WeWork, Awfis)*
