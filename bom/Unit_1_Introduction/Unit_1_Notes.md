## 1. Nature and Purpose of Business

### Meaning & Definition
- **Definition:** An organized economic activity involving the regular production, purchase, or distribution of goods and services undertaken with the motive of earning profits through the satisfaction of consumer wants.
- **Key Characteristics:**
  1. **Economic Activity:** Directed towards the creation of utilities and wealth.
  2. **Production or Procurement of Goods & Services:** Transforming inputs into marketable utilities.
  3. **Sale or Exchange:** Regular transactions for value (not for personal consumption).
  4. **Regularity in Dealings:** Isolated one-time sales do not constitute business.
  5. **Profit Motive:** Primary economic engine ensuring business continuity, capital expansion, and risk reward.
  6. **Element of Risk & Uncertainty:** Subject to market, technological, and competitive volatility.

## 2. Factors to be Considered Before Starting a Business

1. **Selection of the Line of Business:** Product/service choice, market viability, technical feasibility.
2. **Size and Scale of Operation:** Small, medium, or large scale based on capital and market size.
3. **Choice of Legal Form:** Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, LLP, or Joint Stock Company.
4. **Location of the Enterprise:** Raw material access, consumer proximity, logistics, labor, tax incentives.
5. **Capital Structure & Financial Planning:** Sizing fixed capital (assets) and working capital (daily operations).
6. **Physical Facilities & Layout:** Plant layout, machinery selection, ergonomic flow.
7. **Workforce Competence:** Hiring managerial and technical workforce.
8. **Statutory Registrations:** GST, PAN, factory licenses, municipal compliance.

## 4. Modern Business Formats

### A. Brick & Mortar
- **Concept:** Traditional physical storefronts where customers physically enter, touch merchandise, and purchase in person.
- **Merits:** Tactile physical experience, instant possession, personal customer service.
- **Demerits:** High real estate rental costs, restricted local catchment area, limited operating hours.

### B. Brick & Click (Omnichannel)
- **Concept:** Integration of brick-and-mortar storefronts with digital e-commerce web/mobile platforms.
- **Key Feature:** BOPIS (Buy Online, Pick Up In Store), localized web fulfillment, easy in-store returns.
- **Merits:** Broadest market reach, high customer trust, reduced delivery logistics friction.
- **Demerits:** High dual overheads, channel pricing conflicts, complex IT inventory synchronization.

### C. E-Commerce Models
- **B2B (Business-to-Business):** Indiamart, Alibaba.
- **B2C (Business-to-Consumer):** Amazon, Flipkart.
- **C2C (Consumer-to-Consumer):** OLX, eBay.
- **C2B (Consumer-to-Business):** Freelancers bidding on Upwork for enterprise projects.

### D. Franchising
- **Concept:** Contractual licensing where **Franchisor** grants **Franchisee** operational rights to its business model, brand name, and proprietary systems for fees and recurring royalties.
- **Merits for Franchisor:** Fast capital-light global scaling, motivated owner-operators.
- **Demerits for Franchisor:** Risk of brand damage by low-standard franchisees, continuous monitoring costs.
- **Merits for Franchisee:** High survival rate, established brand trust, operational playbook.
- **Demerits for Franchisee:** Strict loss of autonomy, mandatory royalty payments on gross revenues.

### E. Outsourcing (BPO / KPO)
- **BPO (Business Process Outsourcing):** Contracting operational back-office/front-office tasks (customer care, payroll, technical support) to external specialists.
- **KPO (Knowledge Process Outsourcing):** Contracting high-end intellectual tasks requiring advanced domain expertise (financial modeling, legal research, data analytics).

## 5. Nature and Functions of Management

### Classic Definitions
- **Harold Koontz:** *"Management is the art of getting things done through and with people in formally organized groups."*
- **Henry Fayol:** *"To manage is to forecast and to plan, to organise, to command, to coordinate and to control."*

### Functions of Management (POSDCORB Overview)
1. **Planning:** Goal-setting, forecasting, determining future course of action.
2. **Organizing:** Defining roles, grouping activities, assigning responsibility and authority.
3. **Staffing:** Recruitment, selection, training, performance appraisal, compensation.
4. **Directing:** Guiding, supervising, motivating, and leading personnel.
5. **Controlling:** Setting performance standards, measuring output, correcting deviations.

## 6. Managerial Competencies (Robert Katz's Skill Model)

```
        TOP MANAGEMENT       ──►  [ Conceptual Skills ]   (Strategy, vision)
       MIDDLE MANAGEMENT     ──►  [ Human Skills ]        (Empathy, motivation)
       LOWER / SUPERVISORY   ──►  [ Technical Skills ]    (Operational methods)
```
- **Technical Skills:** Specialized knowledge and operational techniques.
- **Human / Interpersonal Skills:** Communicating, resolving conflicts, and inspiring teamwork.
- **Conceptual Skills:** Holistic systems thinking, diagnosing environmental trends, corporate vision.
